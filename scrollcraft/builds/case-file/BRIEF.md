## Grammar: Access Path (new)

The page is an authorization chain, taken from Cameron's own Phase 1 model:
**Identity → Privilege → Scope → Lifecycle → Validation**.

- **Nav:** a clickable path rail (left margin on desktop, bottom bar on phone)
  naming the five steps; each step lights as the visitor passes it.
- **Sequence:** hard-cut chapters on alternating grounds (studio forest / bone
  paper). No crossfades between chapters, no continuous drift gradient.
- **Hero:** a pinned character stage (bespoke, not a scrub video).
- **Close:** an access request that resolves as approved: the CTA set inside a
  request record, not a button island.
- **Bans:** pinned crossfade type acts outside the hero, card grids, more than
  one pinned act, spotlight, magnetic CTA, stat counters (no real metrics exist).

Why not the eight defined grammars: filmic one-shot hides the seams that let a
recruiter jump to evidence; chaptered editorial forbids the media hero the
brief requires; live surface would mean faking an Entra console (banned);
continuous world has no real geography; typographic poster bans the character;
gallery suits objects, not arguments; split stage needs two sides; cutlist is
the wrong energy for an evaluator.

## Signature move

**A.R.I.A.'s gaze directs the reading.** His head turn is bound to scroll: he
looks at the name, turns to meet the visitor as the specialization lands, then
turns and steps aside to present the access path, which appears exactly where
he is looking. Reverse scroll reverses it.
