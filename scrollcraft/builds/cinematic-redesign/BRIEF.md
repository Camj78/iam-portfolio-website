Supersedes the presentation layer of `builds/case-file/BRIEF.md`; the Access
Path concept, palette, A.R.I.A. and the evidence rules carry over.

## Cameron's direction (2026-10-20, summarized from his message)

- The Milestone 1 site "feels like a long-form technical documentation page":
  too wordy, too vertically stacked, no cinematic layering or depth.
- Wanted: a substantial visual redesign, A.R.I.A. as the dominant subject in a
  dimensional environment, at least four independently animated layers,
  oversized type interacting with the character, a pinned scroll transition,
  and a clear reveal into a CFG-based Identity section. Cut homepage copy by
  roughly 60 to 70 percent.
- **CFG only.** GHOSTD is excluded from the rendered site and from skills
  evidence. Nothing planned is shown as done.
- Keep: palette (charcoal green, tan, cream, emerald), the three A.R.I.A. poses
  (no Kling spend yet), the Access Lounge cover, the Access Path.
- Process: vertical slice first (hero + Identity transition), screenshots,
  approval before any further stage. No push or deploy without approval.

## Grammar change

Milestone 1 banned more than one pinned act and called for hard-cut
chapters. This direction asks for pinned cinematic scenes and smooth section
handoffs, so those bans are lifted for the redesign. The hero no longer
hard-cuts: the pin releases onto the same ground as its final frame.
